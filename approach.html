THE COORDINATE PLANE OF CONSCIOUSNESS

What you choose sends consciousness up or down

The x-axis represents ‘action’ driven by good thoughts, and the y-axis represents ‘consciousness’ grown through those actions. Depending on your choice (+ or −) every time life tests you, your consciousness rises into Plus Zone or falls into Minus Zone.

Category
Consciousness Zone
State of Mind and Behavioral Patterns
Plus (+) Zone
Truthfulness, honesty, sincerity, diligence
love, consideration, understanding, forgiveness
initiative, positivity, proactivity, creativity…
Wherever you go, there is a place to live, and blessings begin right here.
No matter how hard it gets, never go back to the minus, but choose Plus again.
Zero (0) Zone
A state of neither love nor hate.
A state of alternating between love and hate.
No change (+1−1=0)
Good deeds without reflection leave consciousness unchanged.
A single wrong choice can easily drop you back into Minus Zone.
Minus (−) Zone
Lies, deception, laziness, hatred, resentment, complaints,
idleness, complacency, arrogance…
Wherever you go, there is no place to stay, and even coming blessings run away.
Saying “It’s because of this that I ended up like this,” you keep choosing Minus.

The Calculus of Life — Every momentary choice (dy/dx) accumulates (∫) to shape the trajectory of your entire life.

SOLUTION I

Higher Growth

When encountering an event or a person, reflect on (differentiate) whether your thoughts and actions at that moment were a Plus choice or a Minus choice. If it was a Minus, convert it into a Plus and put it into practice—then your consciousness will grow to a higher level than before.

SOLUTION II

Higher Rapport

Higher Rapport is a sustainable relationship of mutual growth. By redesigning how we view feedback, it turns every stimulus into a learning opportunity, leading to shared growth with one another.

SOLUTION III

Three-Step 3S Questioning Method

The 3-Step Questioning Method is a core approach in PLUSZONE Education, enabling you to see yourself objectively, clarify your direction, and take ownership of your life.

STEP 1
Self-Reflection
Question“Why did I think and act that way?”
Expected EffectDiscovering the root causes of unconscious thoughts and emotions
STEP 2
Setting Plus (+) Standards
Question“Are these thoughts and words of mine Plus or Minus?”
Expected EffectIndependently reviewing and judging value standards to autonomously choose values to put into practice
STEP 3
Standards into Action
Question“Then what should I do now, and how?”
Expected EffectNaturally translating the standards one has judged to be right into actual action
