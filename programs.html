TEACHER TRAINING

“Becoming a Plus 美人: Growing by Practicing Character”

Goal: build a system of teacher reflection and fully master the four processes in the field

1Why Character EducationCharacter education is about real change in daily life. Value standards that aren’t applied to life remain mere knowledge.
2Higher RapportLearn Higher Rapport: a state of trust, one step beyond rapport, built on educational love.
3Elevate YourselfCharacter education starts with me. Practice detailed methods of teacher self-reflection and growth in consciousness.
4Diagnosing with QuestionsDiagnosis begins with questions and listening. Are you asking out of real curiosity, without prejudice?
5Setting Value StandardsOne-way lectures, NO! Awakening through questions, YES! Teachers set their own value standards first.
6The Three-Step Questioning MethodStep 1 Value Recognition → Step 2 Clarifying Value Standards → Step 3 Applying Values
7Solidifying Value StandardsChange in life comes from habits. Consistent follow-up moves students from external to internal motivation.
HOW IT WORKS

The joy of growth by choosing PLUS (+)

Everyone wants to live happily. PLUSZONE Education helps people discover the minus that creates the gap between who they want to be and who they are now, and experience the joy of choosing plus for themselves.

STEP 01Diagnosing Value Standards

Today is the integral of everything up to yesterday: my life graph.

  • Discovering what has held you back
  • Past vs. future
STEP 02Setting Value Standards

Not a right answer: you discover and choose the plus yourself.

  • Turning what held you back into a stepping stone for growth
  • Making one value standard the goal of your life
STEP 03Solidifying Value Standards

For those who want to grow, every event is a chance to grow.

  • Discover it in the field, win in the field: a Great Victory
  • The path to growth that feedback opens

All of this takes place on Higher Rapport: trust built on helping and caring for one another.
