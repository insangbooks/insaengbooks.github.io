THE COORDINATE PLANE OF CONSCIOUSNESS

What you choose sends consciousness up or down

The x-axis is “action” drawn out by good thoughts; the y-axis is “consciousness” that grows through that action. Each time life collides with you, what you choose (+ or −) sends consciousness up into the Plus Zone or down into the Minus Zone.

Zone
Values in this zone
State of mind and behavior
Plus (+) Zone
Truthfulness, honesty, sincerity, diligence
Love, consideration, understanding, forgiveness
Proactivity, positivity, initiative, creativity…
Wherever you go, there is a place to live, and blessings begin here.
No matter how hard it gets, you never sink into the minus. You choose plus again.
Zero (0) Zone
Neither love nor hate
Loving, then hating, then loving again
No change (+1−1=0)
You do good without reflecting, so consciousness stays where it is and does not grow.
One wrong choice and it is easy to slide back into the minus.
Minus (−) Zone
Falsehood, deceit, laziness, hatred, resentment, complaint,
idleness, complacency, arrogance…
Wherever you go, there is no place to stay, and even blessings on their way slip away.
Saying “I ended up like this because of…”, you keep choosing minus.

The calculus of life — each moment’s choice (dy/dx) accumulates (∫) and determines the trajectory of your whole life.

SOLUTION I

Higher Growth

When you collide with an event or a person, reflect on (differentiate) whether your thoughts (heart) and actions in that moment were a plus or a minus choice. If minus, turn it into plus and put it into practice, and your consciousness grows to a higher level than before.

SOLUTION II

Higher Rapport

Higher Rapport redesigns how we see feedback, so that any stimulus is absorbed as learning and we grow together with the other person: a lasting rapport of mutual growth.

SOLUTION III

The 3S Questioning Method

The three-step questioning method helps you see yourself objectively, set a clear direction, and complete your life as its author. It is the core questioning method of PLUSZONE Education.

STEP 1
Self-Reflection
Question“Why did I think and act that way?”
Expected effectDiscover the causes of unconscious thoughts and feelings
STEP 2
Setting Plus (+) Standards
Question“Are these thoughts and words of mine plus or minus?”
Expected effectExamine and judge your value standards yourself, and choose the values you will practice
STEP 3
Standards into Action
Question“So what should I do now, and how?”
Expected effectNaturally carry the standard you judged right into real action
