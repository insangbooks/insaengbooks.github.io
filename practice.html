Self-ReflectionWhy did I think and act that way?

Setting Plus (+) StandardsAre my thoughts and words Plus or Minus?

What can you do?

FEATURE 1

Question Practice

Pick a situation card and answer the three questions in turn. At each step you get AI feedback on what to keep and what to change.

FEATURE 2

Concern Analysis

Write the situation, the child’s response, the question you asked and the result. The app reads it in three steps and suggests the next conversation flow and example questions.

Privacy — What you enter is used only to generate AI feedback and is not stored. Your result PDF is created on your own device. Only cases you agree to share for improving the method are kept separately, anonymized with names and school names removed. If you sign up for our newsletter, your email is used only to send it and is kept for one year from sign-up, then deleted. Up to 8 uses per day. Privacy Policy
