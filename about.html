ABOUT

Two teachers who found answers in the classroom

The roots of PLUSZONE Education are not in theory but in the classroom. The answers two teachers found through 34 and 22 years of working with children became the starting point of this education.

School health teacher, 34 years

Kim Kyeong-Hyi

School Health TeacherBachelor’s Degree in NursingMaster of Education in Counseling Education

Studied nursing at university and counseling education in graduate school. Caring not only for the bodies but also for the hurting hearts of children who come to the health room, has seen children who used to ask for medicine for stomachaches and headaches recover so fully that they no longer needed it—through character education that helps them change a single thought and a single feeling.

Believing that teachers must first have good character in order to teach it, applies this to themself first every day and walks the path of growing together with students.

Elementary school teacher, 22 years

Kim Hye-Jin

Elementary School TeacherMaster of Education in Educational CounselingCompleted Doctoral Coursework in Education

Graduated from a university of education, earned a Master of Education in educational counseling and completed doctoral coursework in education. Dreamed of a happy teaching life, but reality was not easy: children who swore at teachers, and parents who overprotected or neglected. Has worked to find ways to teach character “without burning out.”

Keeps records of experiences with children, explores how teachers, students and parents can all stand upright in character, and moves forward step by step with the resolve “I will become a right person first.”

OUR PHILOSOPHY

The joy of growth by choosing PLUS (+)

Everyone wants to live happily. PLUSZONE Education helps people discover the minus that creates the gap between who they want to be and who they are now, and experience the joy of choosing plus for themselves.

(+,+): the growth of consciousness — Education that shows how your life becomes plus (+) when you meet every thought and way of living actively, proactively and positively.
A SINGLE THOUGHT

A Single Thought

Here is a single thought.
A single thought gives rise to a person’s words.
A person’s words give rise to their actions.
Their actions give rise to their habits.
Their habits shape the atmosphere of a place.

The atmosphere of a place
shapes the values and customs
of a family, a workplace, and a society.
The values and customs of a society
shape the history of an era.

Therefore, ask yourself:
What kind of thought are you holding?
What kind of words are you speaking?
What kind of actions are you taking?
Look back and reflect.

Know that the single thought
you are holding at this very moment
is shaping your destiny
and creating your future and your history.

With what single thought are you making history?
With what single thought
are you painting the picture of your life?

Are you following others and copying their pictures?
Or are you painting a picture of your own?
Are you painting a picture that is stained and ugly?
Or one filled with beauty and grace?
Or are you merely dressing it up for appearances?

With a single thought,
I have painted the picture of my life.
With clarity and meticulous care,
I have shaped every detail of my life —
making each and every step
joyful and beautiful.

written by Sang Dae Lee
