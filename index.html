THE COORDINATE PLANE OF CONSCIOUSNESS

What you choose sends
consciousness up or down

The x-axis represents ‘action’ driven by good thoughts, and the y-axis represents ‘consciousness’ grown through those actions. Depending on the choices (+, −) you make in every encounter, your consciousness rises into the Plus Zone or falls into the Minus Zone.

The Calculus of Life — Every momentary choice (dy/dx) accumulates (∫) to shape the trajectory of your entire life.

Plus (+)ZONE

Truthfulness, honesty, love, consideration, forgiveness. No matter how hard it gets, you choose Plus again.

Zero (0)ZONE

Neither love nor hate (+1−1=0). Without reflection, consciousness stays where it is.

Minus (−)ZONE

Lies, laziness, resentment, complaints. Blaming circumstances, you keep choosing Minus.

Plus, Zero and Minus are not labels for a child or for feelings; they are standards for reflecting on our thoughts and choices in the moment. We do not divide feelings into good or bad—we help children understand their feelings and then choose their next thoughts and actions for themselves.

STEP 1 · UNDERSTANDTHREE SOLUTIONS

The power to choose Plus
every time life tests you

SOLUTION I

Higher Growth

When you encounter an event or a person, reflect on (differentiate) whether your thoughts and actions at that moment are making a Plus choice or a Minus choice. If it is a Minus, convert it into a Plus and put it into practice—then your consciousness will grow to a higher level than before.

SOLUTION II

Higher Rapport

A sustainable relationship of mutual growth. It redesigns how we view feedback, turning every stimulus into a learning opportunity. Each cycle of rapport → feedback → relationship crisis → crisis resolution builds a higher level of shared growth.
